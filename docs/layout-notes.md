# Bay station PCB -- placement notes (placement only, nothing routed)

Status 2026-11-18. `bay-station/bay-station.kicad_pcb` now carries the board outline, 4 M3 holes,
3 fiducials and a position for all 428 footprints. **There are no tracks, no vias and no filled
zones.** Everything is generated by one idempotent script:

```
/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/Current/bin/python3 \
    scripts/layout/place_bay_station.py [--dump layout.json] [--out other.kicad_pcb] [--no-save]
python3 scripts/layout/render_layout.py layout.json out.png [--crop x0 y0 x1 y1] [--nets uwb] [--labels]
```

The script resets nothing it does not own: it keeps the footprints, nets, 4-layer stackup and the
`.kicad_pro` (restored byte for byte after `BOARD.Save()`, which otherwise wipes the netclasses;
memory note *kicad-pcbnew-headless-gotchas*), seeds the KIIDs, and a second run reproduces the file
exactly (md5 checked). Hand-placed parts (ICs, connectors, SMA, RF chains, mechanical) are the
`FIXED` table plus `chain_positions()`; every passive and test pad is then placed by a
deterministic greedy search (`Placer.greedy`) that pulls it toward the pin it serves, inside a
per-block region, on a 0.25 mm grid (test pads: 1.25 mm grid), courtyards inflated by 0.3 mm so
neighbouring courtyards are >= 0.6 mm apart (room for silk and stitching vias).

Coordinates below are board-local mm, origin at the top-left corner, x east, y south. The board
origin sits at (60, 50) on the KiCad sheet.

## Block rationale

### UWB array (`uwb_array_notes.md`, `positioning.md`)

* **Why 2 x 2 with SMA on two edges, not a row of four.** Clock-path equality was the deciding
  metric: 1 cm of differential path is ~10 cm of position error at 10 m (positioning.md; CLAUDE.md).
  A row of four on one edge forces buffer-to-XTI paths that differ by one chip pitch (>= 22 mm with
  the cluster width the decoupling needs). A brute-force search over the 24 anchor-to-slot
  assignments x 4 buffer rotations (done offline, result frozen in `SLOT`) gave the 2 x 2 layout the
  shortest, non-crossing tree with a path spread of ~10 mm instead of ~22. The price is two coax
  exits (north and south) and a block that splits the board into west (UWB) and east (host/power);
  west-east nets cross through the centre band.
* **RF path (identical on all four).** Straight line chip RF1 pin 18 -> C47 (2.0 pF) -> R220 (0R)
  -> J2x pad 1, with the DNP pi shunts C200/C201 beside the line, all 0402 per
  `uwb_array_notes.md` "RF front end". Estimated pad-centre chain length is **9.02 mm for A0, A1, A2
  and A3** (spread 0.00 mm); the north chains are the 180-degree image of the south chains. RF2 pin
  13 gets its 49R9 (R33-R36) directly below/above the pin. Nothing but the 0402s sits in the
  1.5 mm channel between chip and SMA, so a 50 ohm line of the stackup calculator's width fits.
  The chain stays 4.25 mm from the chip centre outward so no part touches the chip courtyard.
* **Clock tree (`clock_dist_notes.md` "Layout").** U31 sits at the centre of the block; R231-R234
  (22R) are at the buffer pins and C24-C27 (2200 pF) at each XTI pin as the note asks. Straight-line
  buffer-pin -> R -> C -> XTI estimates: A0 30.4, A1 28.8, A2 34.4, A3 24.7 mm (spread 9.7 mm).
  **That spread has to be closed with a meander on A3 and A1 (~10 and ~6 mm) at routing**; a fixed
  mismatch calibrates out, a long unequal run that drifts with temperature does not. No ratsnest
  crossing remains in the tree (11 before). TCXO output pin to U31 CLKIN is 8 mm.
* **Decoupling (Fig 32 via the `Placement` fields in `uwb_array.kicad_sch`).** Per anchor the greedy
  pass pulls C45 to VTX_D pin 27, C41 to VDD2a pin 28 (FB after it), C40 to VDD1 pin 29, C42/C43/C44
  to VDD3 pin 26 (smallest first), C46 to VIO_D pin 38, C28 to XTO pin 22 and C24 to XTI pin 21,
  all inside a 25 x 20 mm box around the chip. They sit 1-8 mm from their pins; the packing limit
  (0603 courtyard 3 x 1.5 mm) means the 4.7 uF/10 uF parts are the furthest. Verify against Fig 32
  once routed.
* **No copper under the chip.** Rule areas `KO_DW3210_An_body` forbid tracks, vias and pours
  under each body on F.Cu and In1.Cu (decisions.md says the Fig 35 photo shows In1 cleared only
  under the RF half; the brief asked for both layers, so both are blocked -- relax In1 if routing
  needs it).
* **A0 fallback crystal Y1 (DNP)** and C208/C209 are at U4's XTI/XTO pins.

### Clock sources

TCXO U30 is the 15.6 x 10.6 mm PLACEHOLDER footprint (Rakon RPT1490LN, quote-only part). Pin 4
(output) faces the buffer; the pad geometry is unverified, so re-check the 8 mm output run when the
real footprint arrives.

### Magnetometer U10 / accelerometer U11 (decisions.md "Environment and georeferencing")

U11 is 5.5 mm from U10. Measured from the placed board: U10 is 68.7 mm from L1, 51.3 mm from
L300 (guideline >= 25 mm), 27.7-33.1 mm from the nRF9151 supply bulk caps C100-C103 (>= 20 mm),
46.5 mm from U40, 25.2 mm from the TCXO can, >= 31 mm from every M3 hole, 44.6 mm from the SIM
shell, 80+ mm from the jacks and battery. **The +VBAT_LTE and +VSYS routes must be kept >= 20 mm
from U10 at routing** (U40 is 46.5 mm from U10; keep the U40 -> nRF9151 feed east of x = 60). The 25/20 mm figures are engineering guidance, not ST numbers.

### Host: nRF9151 U20, BT840 U3, SIM, RF (`connectivity_notes.md`)

* U20 is rotated 0: its UWB pins (67-80) face west toward the array, the BT840 UART pins face north
  toward U3, SIM/SWD/reset pins face south, LTE (pin 35) faces east. U3 is rotated 0 with its
  antenna end flush with the north edge (antenna assumed at the module's -Y end, from the pad field
  offset in `Fanstel_BT840_Module_14x16mm_16Cast_49LGA`); no other part is within 10 mm of the
  antenna end in x or in the antenna rectangle.
* J10 (LTE) and J11 (GNSS) u.FL are on the north edge, J11 at (79.5, 4.5), J10 at (88, 10.5). The pi at
  J10 (R130/C130/C131/D101) is at the connector as `connectivity_notes.md` asks. The GNSS LNA U24
  sits at the connector (J11 -> LNA input chain 9.5 mm) so the pre-LNA loss is minimal; the LNA
  output run to the nRF9151 pin 42 is 29 mm. The LTE run from pin 35 to J10 is 22 mm. Both are
  longer than ideal because the nRF9151 has to stay beside the UWB array (the 14-signal SPI/IRQ/CS
  bus is the heaviest connection on the board); they need a 50 ohm line and ground stitching.
* U40 (+VBAT_LTE) is east of the nRF9151: U40 VOUT -> R302 -> FB10 -> VDD is 24 mm; its hot loop
  U40-L300 is 10.5 mm.
* SIM J12 (80, 48) is 13 mm from the nRF9151 SIM pins, contact side toward the modem. U22 (SIM
  ESD) is between them. The DNP eSIM U23 is to its east.
* Programming: J3 (SWD) is 6.4 mm from nRF9151 SWDIO; J34, J33 beside it. BT840's J32/J35 are east
  of the module next to its SWD pins. SW11 (RESET_BLE) is beside them; SW2 (RESET_MCU) beside J3.

### Power (`power_bms_notes.md`, `regulation_notes.md`)

Flow runs east edge -> west: J1/J2 (east edge) -> D1/D2 -> U1 charger (112, 34) -> +VSYS -> U8
(104, 48) and U40 (84.5, 33) -> +3V3 west to the array. BT1 JST is on the east edge (battery cable
leaves the board), Q1/D3/U2 next to it. U8: SW pin -> L1 -> C21 is 8.8 mm, input cap C20 2.4 mm from
VIN, feedback divider R8 4.7 mm from FB and not adjacent to the SW pin. Charger LEDs D4-D6 are on
the south edge with their resistors. LEDs D7/D400/D401 and SW12 are on the north edge east of
the u.FL connectors where a lid can show them (**enclosure not designed: all UI positions are
provisional**).

### Test pads (`verification-plan.md` test-point index)

127 pads, D1.0 (107) and D1.5 (20), all on F.Cu, on a 1.25 mm grid; minimum centre distance 3.75 mm
(>= 2.5 mm probe spacing). Each pad is pulled to the pin or net it measures (explicit targets for the
rail and ground-spring pads: TP3/TP110 at the clock, TP111/TP112 beside TP10/TP12, TP101 at the far
end of the array, TP102 at the nRF9151/BT840 end), then a swap pass shortens the worst ones.
TP402/TP411/TP412 are switch-node pads: **routing must put them on the switch copper with no stub or
delete them** (`regulation_notes.md` section 7). Average distance from a signal pad to the nearest other pad on its net is 7.6 mm; the worst are the
shared-bus pads (TP13/16/18/20 for the IRQ/CS lines, 20-30 mm) and a few regulation pads, because
their pin neighbourhoods are full. They are the first thing to trim if the board needs room. The
host pads are placed after the RF and supply parts (placing them first pushed the LTE/GNSS and
+VBAT_LTE passives 5-15 mm further out, which is the worse trade).

per-anchor CS/IRQ nets are 25-54 mm.

## Rotations and conventions

Passives are at 0/90 except where pad order must follow the signal: the vertical RF series parts
(C47, R220) and RF2 terminations (R33-R36) are at 270 (south chips) / 90 (north chips) so pad 1
faces the chip, and the north pi shunts (C202/C203/C206/C207) are at 180 as the mirror of the south
ones. ICs and connectors use 0/90/180/270 (connectors turn their mating face toward the edge).
Passive-group orientation is otherwise whatever the pad-distance cost chose within 0/90 -- it was
not post-normalised to a single polarity convention, which a silk pass will want.

## DRC (`kicad-cli pcb drc`, run from a scratch directory against the repo board)

* courtyards_overlap: **0**; hole/edge errors other than the SMA: **0**.
* copper_edge_clearance: **20 errors, all four SMA footprints x 5 pads**: the Linx pads start at
  the board edge by design (footprint origin on the edge line) and `.kicad_pro` requires 0.5 mm.
  Expected until the SMA launch is redesigned/simulated.
* silk_edge_clearance 6 (BT1 silk at the edge), silk_overlap 199, silk_over_copper 199: reference
  text and silk outlines; the silk script owns these.
* unconnected items: 499 (no routing).
* The stackup, netclass set (only `Default` exists in this project) and `.kicad_pro` are unchanged.

## Not verified / assumptions to check (flag, not invention)

1. **Connector mating faces** (J1 barrel, J2 USB-C, BT1 JST, J12 SIM) are assumed to be the
   footprint's +Y end (pads at the back); no datasheet drawing or 3D model was checked. Turn each
   toward its edge before ordering. SIM slot direction in particular is a guess.
2. **SMA footprint is a PLACEHOLDER** (`SMA_Linx_CONSMA020.042-G_EdgeMount_PLACEHOLDER`): the
   1.52 mm centre pad is far wider than a 50 ohm line and the connector is rated for 1.07 mm
   board vs this 1.048 mm stackup. The launch and ground cut-back need EM simulation at 6.5 GHz.
   The chain lengths above are pad-centre estimates, not electrical lengths.
3. **BT840 antenna side and keep-out size** are inferred from the pad field and a conservative
   10 mm side clearance; Fanstel's drawing was not re-read here.
4. **DW3210 land pattern and the Fig 35 keep-out** are still the unresolved items in decisions.md;
   the 4 x 4 mm rule area is a placement-stage guard, not the verified copper rule.
5. **TCXO, MAX17048 and eSIM footprints are placeholders**, so their courtyards (and U30's 8 mm
   output run) may change.
6. **Clock-path spread of 9.7 mm** needs a meander or a nudge of the A1/A3 clusters; nothing was
   routed to prove the equal-length goal.
7. **LTE and GNSS u.FL are 23-24 mm from the nRF9151 pins**, not adjacent; accepted for the shorter
   SPI bus. Moving the host to the north edge would shorten them at the cost of the bus.
8. **No 3D clearance check**: TCXO height, jack, SIM and u.FL against an enclosure lid are open
   (the enclosure is not designed).
9. **Hole grounding, LED/button locations, header orientations** follow the open decisions and were
   placed for access, not against an enclosure drawing.
10. Test pads were placed for proximity, not yet reviewed against how a spring probe approaches
    each cluster.
