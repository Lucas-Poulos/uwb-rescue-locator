# Bay station power-stage layout (stage 5)

Generated by `scripts/layout/route_power.py`; nets and left-over pads in `scripts/layout/power_nets.json`.
Status: **hot-loop cells of U8 and U40, local decoupling feeds and most rails are routed; several rails are
still partial** (list below). Tested on a scratch copy of the placed board plus `place_testpads_bottom.py`
(not on the board file under version control: no board is committed with this stage).

```
KP=/Applications/KiCad/KiCad.app/Contents/Frameworks/Python.framework/Versions/Current/bin/python3
$KP scripts/layout/route_power.py --board SCRATCH/bay-station.kicad_pcb [--report r.json] [--nets-json x.json] [--no-dist]
```

Run time about 90-150 s. Idempotent: a second run on its own output gives the same md5 (all of its copper is
members of the pcb group `route_power`, removed and rebuilt; movers are ignored as obstacles while they are
re-placed in fixed order). `.kicad_pro` is restored by `pcbutil.save_board`. Zones are not created or filled.

## What it does

1. **Placement tweak** (only power-stage passives; ICs/connectors never move). Each part goes to the legal pose
   (courtyard + 0.3 mm per side, so >= 0.6 mm to every other part; outside no-track rule areas and 0.8 mm from the edge)
   that minimises the distance from its loop pad to the IC pin it serves. Moved: L1, C20, C21, R8, R9, C22, R301 (U8
   cell); L300, C300, C301, C302, R302, R306, R307 (U40 cell); C1-C4 (charger), C102/C103 (nRF9151 VDD), C107/C108
   (VDD_GPIO), C210/C211 (TCXO), C213/C214 (clock buffer), C40-C44, C48-C52, C56-C60, C64-C68 (DW3210 VDD). Every
   move is printed by the script and stored in the `--report` JSON (`moves`); the biggest are C300 (33 mm, it sat 27 mm
   from U40), C1 (28 mm), C2 (17 mm), C20/C21 (15 mm), C102 (18 mm).
2. **Hot loops by explicit geometry** on F.Cu over In1 (h = 0.254 mm), then the feedback and sense nets.
3. **Ground:** every power-ground pad gets >= 2 vias 0.45/0.20 within 1.4-1.6 mm (short 0.25-0.4 mm stub). Vias are
   through, so In1 and In2 both see them; In1 stays unbroken (no copper of this stage is on In1).
4. **Distribution** by A* (0.25 mm grid, 45 degree moves, 0.45 mm chamfers, class widths with a necking ladder,
   via pairs at layer changes: 0.8/0.4 pairs on POWER_HI, 0.45/0.2 otherwise).
5. **RF/CLK corridors:** while an RF50/CLK net has no copper, the straight MST of its pads is reserved on F.Cu at
   0.225 + 0.55 mm (rule is 0.5), except the 1.5 mm next to each pad (the pads are real copper). Real RF50/CLK
   copper is an obstacle at the .kicad_dru 0.5 mm through the model's per-class clearance. No-track rule areas
   (DW3210 cut-outs, BT840 antenna, u.FL keep-outs) are obstacles; vias avoid no-via areas.
6. Power pours: **none created.** The largest steady current is +VSYS 1.2 A (power.md) and 1.0 mm tracks carry
   ~2.4 A at 10 K rise (IPC-2221, docs/routing-plan.md); GND stays on In1/In2.

## Hot loops (loop area and inductance)

Method (all closed form, reproducible with `loop_metrics` in the script): forward path = F.Cu strips, each
`L' = Z0 sqrt(eeff) / c` of a microstrip of that width over In1 (`stackup.microstrip`, h = 0.254, er 4.5), times its
length; return current is in In1 directly under the forward path. Plus vias `L = mu0/(2 pi) h (ln(4h/d)+1) / n` per
return (n parallel vias, only the F.Cu-In1 0.254 mm counts), plus plane spreading between the two via groups
`mu0/(2 pi) h ln(d/r)`, plus the capacitor ESL (**assumed 0.6 nH for an 0805, not a datasheet value**). The
IC's internal bond/die inductance is not included. `A_top` = polygon (cap pad, VIN pad, IC-GND via centroid,
cap-GND via centroid) if the return were on F.Cu; `A_eff = h x (strip length + via-to-via distance)` is the loop
area that carries flux with the In1 return.

| Loop | strip mm | vias (IC/cap) | A_top mm2 | A_eff mm2 | L board nH | + ESL | L total nH | step | spike V |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| U8 TPS62A02 C20-VIN(4)-GND(2) | 3.94 | 2 / 2 | 5.19 | 2.11 | 1.29 | 0.6 | 1.89 | 0.45 A / 2 ns | 0.43 |
| U40 TPS63802 C300-VIN(10)-GND(8) | 3.86 | 2 / 2 | 3.59 | 1.85 | 1.44 | 0.6 | 2.04 | 1.0 A / 2 ns | 1.02 |

Switch-node loops (SW to inductor): U8 SW(3)-L1 pad 1 and U40 L1/L2 (pins 9/7)-L300 are routed as short 0.6-0.8 mm
tracks with 45 degree corners (pin necks 0.25 mm over the first 0.6 mm where the 0.5 mm pad pitch forces it);
their area is not tabulated. The output loops (L - Cout - GND) carry triangular, not pulsed, current.

### Spike budget and assumptions

* **di/dt is a guess.** The repo has no SW rise time. I assumed 2 ns edges (flagged), steps of the load current
  (U8 0.45 A, `regulation_notes.md` s3: 446 mA peak; U40 1.0 A, s2: ~1 A inductor peak). Switching frequencies:
  U8 2.4 MHz (`regulation_notes.md` s7, TPS62A02 SLUSEG9E); U40 not stated in the repo (~2 MHz from memory of
  SLVSEU9D, unchecked).
* U8: 0.43 V spike on +VSYS against 6.5 V abs max (SLUSEG9E Table 6.1) on 5.25 V = 1.25 V margin: passes.
* U40: 1.0 V spike against 6 V abs max (SLVSEU9D s8.1) on 5.25 V = 0.75 V margin: **marginal under these
  assumptions.** Most of it is the assumed 0.6 nH ESL and 2 ns. Mitigation to evaluate at bring-up: a 0.1 uF 0402 right
  at VIN (not in the schematic; flagged, not added); scope the VIN pin, not C300.
* LTE TX load step (+VBAT_LTE, ~0.46 A, 505 mA peak, `regulation_notes.md` s3): feed inductance ~10 nH x 0.46 A / 1 us
  = ~5 mV; droop is set by the control loop and Cout, not layout. Not modelled further.
* DC drop uses 1 oz Cu, R = 0.49 mohm/sq at 20 C (about +12 % at 50 C); a 25 mm 0.5 mm track is ~25 mohm, 12 mV at 0.5 A.

## Nets routed vs left

`power_nets.json`: `routed_nets` (pads connected in the largest component), `left_unconnected`.
Stage 6 (`route_manhattan.py`) must skip every net in `routed_nets`. **Left unconnected** (A* found no path within the
class ladder or the corridors/keep-outs blocked it; to be finished by hand or by `route_manhattan.py --only`):
+5V_IN (the whole net: U1 IN pins, J2, D2, C1, R4, R31, TP104), +3V3_SNS (orientation sensors), most of
+3V3_A0..A3 (FB1.1 and DW3210 VDD pins 26/29 on A0/A1/A2/A3 are not tied to the caps on four anchors), GNSS_LNA_VCC (U24),
+3V3 sinks C212, R104, R202, R203, R411, +3V3_TCXO C211. Ground-via failures: C211.2, C60.2, C67.2. Pads of test
points on routed nets are connected only when the net routing reaches them (see the JSON). Several hot-loop sub-connections
failed in the L/Z shape step and were left to the A* (`FAIL connect ...` lines): U8 PG pull-up path, U40 feed
R302-C301, C301-C302 links, and the DW3210 cap-to-pin links; they are listed in the report `failures`.

## Honest uncertainties

* 2 ns edge, 0.6 nH ESL, 100 kHz-class control loop: assumptions. Inductances are closed-form, not field-solved.
* Placement tweaks pull parts by courtyard rule only; some cells are not pretty (U40 inductor on the right,
  trunks on B.Cu with via pairs next to the pads). The A* trunks have no length/coupling cost to the signal nets.
* The corridor estimate is a straight line between pads (stage 4 meanders CLK up to 10 mm); real RF/CLK copper
  later is obstacle-checked only if stage 4 runs before this script.
